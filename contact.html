Contact Us

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? We would like to hear from you.

Email us

The best way to reach ToolSphere is by email:

toolsphere.contact@gmail.com

What to write to us about

  • Bug reports. Tell us which tool or page, what you did, what you expected, and which browser and device you used.
  • Tool or article ideas. Tell us what would help you.
  • Corrections. If something we published is inaccurate, let us know so we can fix it.
  • Privacy questions. See our Privacy Policy or ask us directly.
Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information by email, even to test a bug. Describe the problem instead. We will never ask you for your passwords.

We read every message and reply as soon as we can. We cannot always reply to every message.
