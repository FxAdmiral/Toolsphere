Privacy Policy

How ToolSphere handles information when you use the site.

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what information ToolSphere ("we", "us") handles when you use this website. We have written it to describe what the site actually does today.

1. Information the tools process

ToolSphere's tools, including the Password Generator, the Password Strength Checker and the Hash Generator, run in your web browser on your own device. The text, passwords and other input you enter are not sent to our servers and are not stored by us.

2. Accounts

ToolSphere does not currently offer accounts or sign-up, and we do not ask you for personal details to use the tools.

3. Messages you send us

If you email us at toolsphere.contact@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to read and reply to your message and to improve the site. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

4. Hosting and technical logs

Like most websites, the company that hosts ToolSphere may automatically record standard technical information when pages are requested, such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages visited. This is used to run, secure and troubleshoot the service and is handled under the hosting provider's own policies.

5. Cookies, analytics and advertising

ToolSphere does not currently use cookies for tracking or advertising, and does not currently run analytics or advertisements.

We may add services such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense in the future. If we do, they may use cookies or similar technologies, and we will update this policy to explain what is collected, why, and how you can control it before or when those services are switched on.

6. Links to other websites

ToolSphere may link to other websites. We do not control them and are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their policies.

7. Children

ToolSphere is a general technology website and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices

If you have emailed us and would like us to delete your message or answer a question about your data, write to toolsphere.contact@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last revised.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at toolsphere.contact@gmail.com or through the Contact page.
