About URL encoding

Web addresses can only contain a limited set of characters. Spaces, accents, emoji and symbols such as & and ? must be replaced by a percent sign and a code, for example a space becomes %20. This is called percent-encoding.

Which button should I use? Use URL part for one piece of an address, such as a search term or a value in a query. It also encodes characters like /, ?, & and =. Use full address for a whole link that you want to keep working: it leaves those structural characters alone.

Decoding turns %20 back into a space. A plus sign is treated as a space, the way browsers do for form data. Encoding is not encryption: anyone can decode it.
