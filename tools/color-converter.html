About color formats and names

                        

                            The same color can be written several ways, and many have a name. Browsers understand 148 standard color names, such as tomato, navy and gold. If your code has no exact name, the tool shows the closest one and says "about". HEX (#2563eb) is the most common in CSS.
                            RGB gives the amounts of red, green and blue from 0 to 255.
                            HSL gives the hue (0 to 360 degrees), saturation and lightness, which is easier to adjust by hand.
                        

                        

                            The contrast figures compare your color with white and black text. Accessibility guidelines (WCAG)
                            ask for at least 4.5:1 for normal text (AA) and 7:1 for the stricter AAA level, so people with low vision can read it.
